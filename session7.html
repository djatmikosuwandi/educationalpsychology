02
Theoretical Essence Hakikat Teori

The Essence of Humanistic Learning Theory Hakikat Teori Pembelajaran Humanistik

Unlike Behaviorism (which views learning as measurable behavioral change shaped by stimulus-response conditioning) and Cognitivism (which treats the learner as an information-processing system similar to a computer), Humanistic Learning Theory conceptualizes learning as a process of humanizing the human being (humanisasi).

Berbeda dengan Behaviorisme (yang memandang belajar sebagai perubahan perilaku terukur akibat stimulus-respons) dan Kognitivisme (yang memproses informasi seperti komputer), Teori Pembelajaran Humanistik memandang belajar sebagai proses memanusiakan manusia (humanisasi).

🌱

Self-Actualization Aktualisasi Diri

Learning is deemed successful when the student deeply understands both their surrounding world and their unique self-potential. Belajar dianggap berhasil apabila siswa memahami lingkungannya dan dirinya sendiri secara optimal.

❤️

Holistic Integration Integrasi Holistik

Education harmonizes cognitive capability with affective elements, emotions, core values, and human free will. Pembelajaran tidak hanya kognitif, melainkan menyatukan aspek afektif, emosi, nilai, dan kehendak bebas (free will).

🤝

Empathetic Facilitator Fasilitator Empatik

Educators serve as facilitators cultivating a safe, empathetic, non-threatening climate for optimal growth. Pendidik bertindak sebagai fasilitator yang menciptakan iklim aman, empati, dan tidak mengancam (non-threatening).

Comparative Spectrum: 3 Core Learning Paradigms Spektrum Komparasi: Tiga Paradigma Pembelajaran

Behaviorism Watson, Skinner

Learner Metaphor:Metafora Pembelajar: Reactive responder conditioned by external stimuli. Organisme pasif yang dikondisikan oleh rangsangan luar.

Core Focus:Fokus Inti: Measurable behavior change, drills, external reinforcement. Perubahan perilaku terukur, drill berulang, reward eksternal.

EdTech: Proctoring tracking, streak badges, flashcard drill apps. Tekpen: Software pengawas, lencana streak kuis, aplikasi hafalan repetitif.
Cognitivism Miller, Sweller

Learner Metaphor:Metafora Pembelajar: Information processor organizing mental schemas. Pemroses informasi aktif pengembang struktur kognitif.

Core Focus:Fokus Inti: Working memory capacity, cognitive load reduction, encoding. Kapasitas memori kerja, efisiensi beban kognitif, skema ingatan.

EdTech: Clean UI, Mayer's multimedia design, chunked microlearning. Tekpen: Desain multimedia Mayer, segmentasi materi, reduksi beban antarmuka.
Humanism Maslow, Rogers, Combs

Learner Metaphor:Metafora Pembelajar: Autonomous whole person pursuing self-actualization. Manusia seutuhnya berkehendak bebas menuju aktualisasi diri.

Core Focus:Fokus Inti: Affective climate, personal meaning, psychological safety, choice. Iklim afektif, pemaknaan personal, keamanan emosional, otonomi.

EdTech: UDL choice, supportive empathetic feedback, community social LMS. Tekpen: Opsi asesmen UDL, umpan balik konstruktif-empatik, komunitas belajar.

Pioneering Theorists Tokoh Utama & Pemikiran

Key Figures & Core Humanistic Principles Tokoh Utama & Prinsip Inti Teori Humanistik

1908 – 1970

Abraham Maslow: Hierarchy of Needs & Self-Actualization

Deficiency Needs vs. Growth Needs Kebutuhan Dasar vs. Pertumbuhan

Maslow asserted that learners cannot attain optimal cognitive performance or reach growth needs (intellectual curiosity, self-actualization) if their fundamental deficiency needs (physiological sustenance, emotional safety, belongingness, and esteem) remain unfulfilled.

Maslow menegaskan bahwa seseorang tidak dapat mencapai fungsi kognitif/pembelajaran optimal (kebutuhan pertumbuhan / growth needs) jika kebutuhan dasar (deficiency needs) seperti rasa aman, pangan, dan keterikatan sosial belum terpenuhi.

Instructional & EdTech Implication:Implikasi Instruksional & Tekpen: Before demanding intensive academic tasks, digital learning spaces must guarantee physical comfort, zero intimidation, and an authentic sense of belonging. Sebelum memberikan materi akademik yang berat, pendidik dan lingkungan digital wajib memastikan kebutuhan fisiologis, keamanan emosional, dan rasa diterima (sense of belonging) siswa terpenuhi.

1902 – 1987

Carl Rogers: Person-Centered & Experiential Learning

Unconditional Positive Regard Penerimaan Tanpa Syarat

Rogers proposed that every human possesses an innate curiosity and drive to learn. Significant learning takes place when content is personally relevant, tied to students' self-chosen goals, and integrates both emotional feeling and experiential action.

Rogers meyakini bahwa manusia memiliki dorongan alami untuk belajar (desire to learn). Belajar yang bermakna (significant learning) terjadi ketika materi relevan dengan tujuan personal siswa, serta melibatkan perasaan dan tindakan langsung (experiential).

The 3 Facilitator Core Conditions (Rogers): Tiga Sikap Fasilitator Utama (Rogers):

1. Genuineness / Realness Authenticity. The educator sheds authoritarian masks and engages openly and honestly. Tampil apa adanya dan jujur tanpa kepalsuan figur kekuasaan kaku.
2. Prizing & Trust Unconditional Positive Regard. Valuing every learner as a person of intrinsic worth. Menerima dan mempercayai potensi siswa tanpa syarat apa pun.
3. Empathetic Understanding Seeing the educational experience through the student's eyes without premature judgment. Empati mendalam terhadap cara pandang siswa tanpa menghakimi.

1912 – 1999

Arthur Combs: Perception & Meaningful Meaning

The Phenomenological Field Medan Fenomenologis & Makna Pribadi

Arthur Combs established that an individual's behavior is the direct outcome of how they perceive themselves and their world at that precise instant (the phenomenological field). Learning does not truly happen when teachers simply dispense facts; real learning occurs when students discover personal meaning: "Why does this knowledge matter to my life?"

Arthur Combs menekankan bahwa perilaku seseorang merupakan hasil langsung dari bagaimana ia mempersepsikan diri dan dunianya pada saat itu (phenomenological field). Belajar bukan sekadar menerima transfer materi baku; belajar sejati terjadi jika materi memiliki makna personal (personal meaning): "Mengapa materi ini penting bagi hidup saya?"

Instructional Rule:Prinsip Desain: Never push curriculum without guiding learners to anchor it to their personal life questions, career aspirations, or immediate communal dilemmas. Pendidik tidak boleh memaksakan materi tanpa membantu siswa menemukan relevansi personal antara topik pembelajaran dengan realitas kehidupannya.

Deci & Ryan (2000)

Self-Determination Theory (SDT) Self-Determination Theory (SDT)

Provides empirical validation for learner ownership through three basic psychological needs: Memberikan landasan empiris bagi konsep kepemilikan belajar melalui 3 kebutuhan psikologis dasar:

  • A Autonomy: Sense of volition and choice over learning pathways.Kendali atas pilihan dan kebebasan arah belajar mandiri.
  • C Competence: Feeling capable through progressive mastery scaffolding.Rasa mampu menguasai keterampilan melalui tantangan terukur.
  • R Relatedness: Genuine interpersonal connection in learning communities.Keterikatan hangat dan relasi saling mendukung dengan sesama.
Vygotsky & Bruner

Social Constructivism Konstruktivisme Sosial

Supplies the epistemological mechanism: knowledge is constructed actively through social dialogues and scaffolding. Menyediakan mekanisme epistemologis: pengetahuan dibangun secara aktif dan kolaboratif, bukan dihafalkan pasif.

Synthesis:Sintesis: While humanism guarantees emotional safety and affirmation, constructivism powers active inquiry and peer collaboration. Jika humanisme menjamin rasa aman dan empati, konstruktivisme menyediakan instrumen inkuiri dan kolaborasi teman sebaya.
Martin Seligman

Positive Psychology (PERMA) Psikologi Positif (PERMA)

Focuses on well-being, character strengths, and emotional resilience as bedrock for academic endurance. Berfokus pada kesejahteraan emosional (well-being), ketahanan diri, dan kekuatan karakter siswa.

The PERMA Pillars:Pilar PERMA: Positive Emotion, Engagement, Relationships, Meaning, Accomplishment. Emosi Positif, Keterlibatan (Flow), Relasi Sehat, Kebermaknaan, dan Pencapaian Otentik.

Group 1 Dilemma • Maslow & SDT Lens Kasus Kelompok 1 • Sudut Pandang Maslow & SDT Surveillance & Anxiety

Case Scenario: The High School Math E-Learning Surveillance Crisis Skenario Kasus: Dilema Demotivasi di Kelas E-Learning Matematika SMA

"A high school launched a full e-learning platform for Mathematics. The school implemented an aggressive automated surveillance and proctoring system that continuously tracks student webcam eye movements and imposes automated grade penalties whenever response times lag. After two months, student participation plummeted, 40% of learners reported severe anxiety, and exam performance dropped drastically." "Sebuah SMA meluncurkan platform e-learning penuh untuk mata kuliah Matematika. Sekolah menerapkan sistem pelacakan otomatis (surveillance/proctoring software) yang sangat ketat yang memantau setiap gerak-gerik siswa di depan kamera, serta memberikan penalti otomatis berupa pengurangan nilai jika waktu respons siswa lambat. Setelah 2 bulan, tingkat partisipasi melonjak turun, 40% siswa melaporkan kecemasan hebat, dan hasil ujian justru merosot tajam."

Question 1: Deconstruct the problem using Maslow's Hierarchy and Self-Determination Theory (Deci & Ryan). Which needs were violated? Pertanyaan 1: Bedah permasalahan di atas menggunakan sudut pandang Hierarki Maslow dan Self-Determination Theory (Deci & Ryan)! Kebutuhan apa yang terlanggar?

Exemplar Rubric Key:Kunci Solusi Model Dosen:
  • Maslow Violation: Destroys Psychological Safety (Level 2). When students feel monitored and penalized, their brain operates in fight-or-flight panic; deficiency needs are unmet, making higher cognitive math problem-solving physiologically impossible.
  • SDT Violations: (1) Autonomy is destroyed by robotic surveillance; (2) Competence is crippled by speed penalties that mistake fast clicking for mathematical mastery; (3) Relatedness becomes cold fear between student and institution.

Question 2: As an EdTech specialist, how would you redesign this e-learning experience to cultivate psychological safety and autonomy? Pertanyaan 2: Sebagai pengembang Teknologi Pendidikan, bagaimana Anda merancang ulang (redesign) pengalaman e-learning dan asesmen agar menciptakan rasa aman dan otonomi?

Exemplar Rubric Key:Kunci Solusi Model Dosen:
  • Abolish Intrusive Proctoring: Replace facial tracking with authentic, untimed open-book performance tasks or asynchronous math explanation videos (Flip/video voice notes).
  • Formative Sandbox: Allow unlimited practice attempts without penalty to encourage positive risk-taking and error-driven learning.
  • Self-Paced Navigation: Permit students to set their own study milestones and choose between problem-solving tracks (Scaffolded vs. Advanced).
Group 2 Dilemma • Rogers & UDL Lens Kasus Kelompok 2 • Sudut Pandang Rogers & UDL Standardization vs. Freedom

Case Scenario: Assessment Flexibility vs. Rigid Graduation Standards Skenario Kasus: Fleksibilitas Asesmen & Kebebasan Belajar pada Desain Media Pembelajaran

"A lecturer in Educational Technology seeks to apply Carl Rogers' Person-Centered Learning in the 'Instructional Media Design' course. However, department policy strictly mandates that every student must submit a hand-coded HTML/CSS website at term end as a standard graduation requirement. Non-coding students suffer extreme stress, feeling their unique creative talents—such as instructional storytelling, manual illustration, or educational podcasting—are completely discarded." "Seorang dosen di Program Studi Teknologi Pendidikan ingin menerapkan prinsip Person-Centered Learning ala Carl Rogers pada mata kuliah 'Desain Media Pembelajaran'. Namun, pihak program studi memiliki aturan ketat bahwa seluruh mahasiswa harus menyerahkan portofolio berupa situs web HTML/CSS pada akhir semester sebagai syarat kelulusan baku. Sebagian mahasiswa yang tidak memiliki Latar Belakang Koding mengalami stres berat dan merasa potensi kreatifnya (seperti menulis naskah, menggambar manual, podcast, atau membuat animasi) terabaikan."

Question 1: Analyze the conflict between Carl Rogers' learning freedom and rigid institutional technical requirements. Pertanyaan 1: Analisis benturan antara kebebasan belajar Carl Rogers dengan pembatasan teknis institusional yang kaku!

Exemplar Rubric Key:Kunci Solusi Model Dosen:
  • Institutional Flaw: Confuses Medium with Mastery. The core instructional competency is "designing effective instructional media", not becoming a frontend web developer.
  • Rogers Violation: Replaces Unconditional Positive Regard with conditional judgment ("You only pass if you code"). This suppresses the "Fully Functioning Person" who excels in visual, auditory, or literary modalities.

Question 2: Propose an assessment redesign based on Universal Design for Learning (UDL) that preserves standards while honoring learner self-actualization. Pertanyaan 2: Rancang solusi asesmen berbasis Universal Design for Learning (UDL) agar standar capaian lulusan tercapai tanpa mengorbankan aktualisasi diri mahasiswa!

Exemplar Rubric Key:Kunci Solusi Model Dosen:
  • Universal Design for Learning (UDL - Multiple Means of Expression): Standardize the Instructional Rubric (needs analysis, target user empathy, instructional clarity, feedback loops) rather than the delivery vehicle.
  • Menu of Formats: Permit students to submit their final media as: (A) Interactive Website; (B) Animated Explainer Video; (C) Educational Podcast Series; or (D) Interactive Digital Graphic Novel.
  • Interdisciplinary Synergy: Encourage collaborative team pairings between strong technical coders and strong content artists.
Group 3 Dilemma • Combs & Positive Psychology Lens Kasus Kelompok 3 • Sudut Pandang Combs & Psikologi Positif AI Automation vs. Human Empathy

Case Scenario: Algorithmic Alienation in Adaptive AI Tutoring Skenario Kasus: Alienasi Pembelajaran Berbasis AI Otomatis di SMP

"A newly deployed Adaptive AI Tutoring platform is introduced in a middle school. The algorithmic AI automatically selects modules, administers quizzes, and assigns grades with zero teacher intervention. While standardized test scores showed a minor statistical increase, teachers discovered that students became utterly passive, refused peer discussions, lost empathy toward classmates, and viewed learning merely as 'checking off algorithmic checkboxes on a screen' devoid of personal meaning." "Sebuah platform Adaptive AI Tutoring baru diterapkan di sebuah SMP. AI tersebut secara otomatis menentukan modul, memberikan kuis, dan memberi nilai tanpa campur tangan guru. Meskipun secara kognitif skor ujian terstandardisasi siswa meningkat sedikit, guru menemukan bahwa siswa menjadi pasif, enggan berdiskusi, kehilangan empati antar sesama, dan menganggap belajar sekadar 'menyelesaikan centang tugas di layar' tanpa makna personal."

Question 1: Evaluate this phenomenon through Arthur Combs (Personal Meaning) and Positive Psychology. Why do higher test scores fail to reflect genuine learning? Pertanyaan 1: Evaluasi fenomena di atas menggunakan teori Arthur Combs (Makna Personal) dan Psikologi Positif! Mengapa kenaikan nilai tes belum mencerminkan belajar sejati?

Exemplar Rubric Key:Kunci Solusi Model Dosen:
  • Combs' Diagnostic: Higher test scores indicate mechanical rote efficiency, not internal schema internalization. The facts exist outside the student's phenomenological field without any personal significance.
  • Positive Psychology (PERMA) Collapse: Zero Relationships (R), zero Meaning (M), and false Accomplishment (A). Learning has been dehumanized into transactional machine interactions.

Question 2: How should EdTech designers establish a Human-in-the-Loop model so AI acts as an enabler of self-actualization rather than a tool of dehumanization? Pertanyaan 2: Bagaimana intervensi desainer Teknologi Pendidikan dalam mengintegrasikan peran humanis guru (human-in-the-loop) agar AI bertindak sebagai enabler aktualisasi diri?

Exemplar Rubric Key:Kunci Solusi Model Dosen:
  • AI as Cognitive Assistant, Not Instructor: Delegate diagnostic data crunching to AI, freeing up teacher time for deep Socratic dialogues, emotional counseling, and project mentorship.
  • Collaborative Synthesis Pauses: Program the AI to freeze every 3 units and mandate peer collaborative discussions before unlocking next chapters.
  • Student Reflection Journaling: Require students to write personal voice reflections on how the lesson applies to their lives, reviewed directly by human mentors.
